# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Radius respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and confidential reports.
- Help newcomers understand the project without condescension.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, unsafe instructions, or deceptive artifacts.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents Radius.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure about affected people.

For an ordinary moderation concern, contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. For a confidential concern, use the repository's private **Report a vulnerability** flow and begin the report with “Code of Conduct report”; maintainers will treat it as confidential and route it appropriately.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
